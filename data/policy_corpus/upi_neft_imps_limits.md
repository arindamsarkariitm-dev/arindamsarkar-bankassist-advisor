# UPI / NEFT / IMPS / RTGS — Transfer Limits & Timing

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual limits.*

## UPI

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Per transaction | ₹1,00,000 |
| Per day (across all UPI apps linked to the account) | ₹2,00,000 |
| Transaction count per day | 20 |

Available 24x7, including holidays. No fee for standard peer-to-peer or merchant UPI transfers on a retail savings account.

## IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)

Per-transaction limit ₹5,00,000, available 24x7 including holidays, credited to the beneficiary instantly (typically within seconds).

## NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)

No per-transaction ceiling for retail customers; processed in half-hourly batches, available 24x7 since the RTGS/NEFT continuous settlement upgrade. Funds are typically credited within 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on the batch and beneficiary bank's processing.

## RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)

Minimum transfer amount ₹2,00,000, no upper ceiling; settled individually and immediately, available 24x7.

## Fees

NEFT, IMPS, and RTGS are free of charge for retail internet/mobile banking initiated transfers on a standard savings account. Branch-initiated transfers may attract a service charge per the current Fee Schedule.

## Increasing a limit

Per-transaction or daily limits can be adjusted by the customer through the mobile app (UPI limits) or by visiting a branch with a request form (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS limits on internet banking). **This is a transactional, self-service or in-branch action — this system cannot change any transfer limit and cannot initiate any transfer.** A request to "increase my limit" or "send this transfer for me" should be refused with a clear pointer to the correct channel.
