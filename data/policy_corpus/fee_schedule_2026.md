# Fee Schedule (2026 edition)

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual fees.*

This schedule lists standard fees for retail savings accounts, debit cards, and credit cards, effective 01 Jan 2026, and **supersedes the 2024 edition (`fee-schedule-2024.1`) in full**. This is the current, authoritative version — always prefer it over any older version when both are retrieved.

## Savings account fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Account maintenance (below minimum balance) | ₹500 / quarter |
| Minimum balance requirement | ₹10,000 |
| Duplicate statement (per request) | ₹100 |
| Cheque book (first 25 leaves/year) | Free; ₹5/leaf thereafter |
| Account closure (within 12 months of opening) | ₹500 |

## Debit card fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Annual maintenance fee | ₹295 + GST |
| Replacement card | ₹200 |
| ATM withdrawal — own bank | Free |
| ATM withdrawal — other bank (beyond 4 free/month) | ₹21 per transaction |
| International transaction (POS/ATM) | 3.5% of transaction value |

## Credit card fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Joining fee | ₹500 + GST (waived for first-year-free offers) |
| Annual fee | ₹1,999 + GST |
| Late payment fee | ₹500 flat, if minimum due unpaid by due date |
| Cash advance fee | 2.5% of amount withdrawn, min ₹300 |
| Foreign transaction fee | **3.5% of transaction value**, converted at prevailing card-network rate |
| Over-limit fee | ₹600 |
| Interest on revolving balance | 3.5% per month (approx. 42% p.a.) |

## Effective date

These fees are effective from **01 January 2026** and remain in force until superseded by a later version of this schedule. Any answer quoting a fee for a transaction dated on or after 01 January 2026 must cite this version, not the 2024 edition.
