# Home Loan — Document Checklist & Eligibility Criteria

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual policy.*

## Eligibility criteria (informational only)

| Criterion | Typical requirement |
|---|---|
| Age at application | 23–60 years (salaried), 25–65 years (self-employed) |
| Minimum income | ₹25,000/month (salaried) or ₹4,00,000/year (self-employed, ITR-based) |
| Credit score | 700+ preferred |
| Employment/business continuity | 2+ years salaried, or 3+ years for self-employed business |
| Loan-to-value ratio | Up to 80% of property value for loans up to ₹75 lakh |

These are the bank's published **criteria**, not a decision — meeting them does not guarantee approval, and the system must never tell a customer they "should qualify" or "will be approved." Actual approval is a credit-underwriting decision made by the loan team after full document review.

## Required documents

**Identity & address (all applicants):** PAN card, Aadhaar or another government-issued photo ID, address proof (utility bill/passport/rental agreement, not older than 3 months).

**Income proof — salaried:** last 3 months' salary slips, last 6 months' bank statements (salary account), Form 16 / latest ITR.

**Income proof — self-employed:** last 3 years' ITR with computation of income, last 12 months' business bank statements, GST returns (if applicable), business proof/registration.

**Property documents:** sale agreement or allotment letter, title deed chain, encumbrance certificate, approved building plan, property tax receipts.

**Other:** processing fee (see current Fee Schedule), passport-size photographs, existing loan statements if any (for obligations assessment).

## What the system does with this

The system can list required documents, explain eligibility criteria in general terms, and answer status questions about an existing application via account tools. It cannot approve, pre-approve, or give a probability of approval — any question phrased as "will I get approved" or "how much can I borrow" that goes beyond stating published criteria should be answered with the criteria plus a clear statement that a final decision requires human underwriting review.
