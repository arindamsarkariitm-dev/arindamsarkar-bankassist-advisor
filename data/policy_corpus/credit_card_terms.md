# Credit Card Terms & Conditions

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual terms.*

## Billing cycle and payment

Each card has a fixed monthly billing cycle (the "billing cycle day" recorded on the account). A statement is generated on that day; payment is due 18 days later. The customer may pay the **total amount due** (no interest charged) or the **minimum amount due** (typically 5% of the outstanding balance, subject to a floor of ₹200), in which case interest accrues on the revolving balance at the rate published in the current Fee Schedule.

## Late payment

If even the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, a flat late payment fee (see current Fee Schedule) is charged, and the missed payment may be reported to credit bureaus after the grace period. Two or more consecutive missed minimum payments trigger a review for the Financial Hardship pathway if the customer indicates difficulty paying — see the Financial Hardship Policy.

## Foreign transactions

All transactions made outside India, or in a foreign currency, attract the foreign transaction fee published in the current Fee Schedule, applied to the INR-converted amount. The fee is typically posted as a **separate line item 1–2 days after** the original purchase, once the card network settles the transaction — this is why a customer may see a purchase and its associated fee as two distinct entries with different dates.

## Cash advances

Withdrawing cash against a credit card is treated as a cash advance, not a purchase: it attracts the cash advance fee immediately and begins accruing interest from the transaction date (no interest-free period applies to cash advances).

## Rewards

Reward points accrue on eligible spend categories at a rate defined per card product and are visible in the mobile app. This system can explain how rewards are earned and redeemed but does not process redemptions.

## Annual fee and joining offers

Some cards carry a first-year-free joining offer, waiving the annual fee for the first 12 months from issuance. If an annual fee is charged in error during an active first-year-free period, it should be flagged as a billing dispute for reversal, not disputed as fraud.

## Limits and over-limit fee

Spending above the assigned credit limit is normally blocked at the point of sale. In rare cases where a transaction is authorised over the limit (e.g., due to network float), an over-limit fee applies per the current Fee Schedule.

## Non-transactional note

This system can explain any of the above and quote the customer's own billing figures once retrieved via account tools. It cannot process payments, change credit limits, waive fees, or approve fee reversals — those require escalation to the appropriate human team.
