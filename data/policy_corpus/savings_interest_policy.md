# Savings & Deposit Interest Policy

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual rates.*

## Savings account interest

Interest is calculated on the **daily closing balance** and credited to the account **quarterly** (end of March, June, September, December).

| Balance slab | Rate (p.a.) |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹1,00,000 | 3.00% |
| ₹1,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 3.50% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 4.00% |

## Recurring Deposit (RD)

Fixed monthly instalment over a chosen tenure (6–120 months); current published rate for a 24-month RD is **6.75% p.a.**, compounded quarterly. Premature closure before 6 months forfeits interest entirely; premature closure after 6 months earns interest at the rate applicable for the period actually completed, minus a 1% penalty.

## Fixed Deposit (FD)

Rates vary by tenure, from 5.50% p.a. (7–45 days) up to 7.25% p.a. (1–2 years) for retail deposits below ₹2 crore; senior citizens receive an additional 0.50% across all tenures.

## Tax deducted at source (TDS)

TDS is deducted on interest income exceeding ₹40,000 in a financial year (₹50,000 for senior citizens), at the rate prescribed under prevailing income tax rules, unless a valid Form 15G/15H is on file.

## What the system can state

Interest already credited to a specific account is a customer-specific fact and must be retrieved via account tools, never estimated. General rate slabs, calculation method, and TDS thresholds are general product knowledge and may be answered from this document directly.
