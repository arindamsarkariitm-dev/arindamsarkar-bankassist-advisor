# Debit Card Terms & Conditions

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual terms.*

## Issuance and activation

A debit card is issued automatically with every savings or current account and must be activated via the mobile app or an ATM PIN-set transaction before first use. Cards are valid for 5 years from issuance.

## Usage limits (standard tier)

| Channel | Daily limit |
|---|---|
| ATM cash withdrawal | ₹40,000 |
| POS / e-commerce purchase | ₹1,50,000 |
| Contactless (tap-and-pay), per transaction | ₹5,000 without PIN |

Customers may request a temporary or permanent limit change through the mobile app or by visiting a branch; this is a self-service or in-branch transactional action and is **not** something this system can perform on the customer's behalf.

## Fees

See the current Fee Schedule for annual maintenance, replacement, ATM, and international transaction charges.

## Lost, stolen, or compromised cards

The customer (or the associate on their behalf, once identity is verified through standard procedure) can block a card instantly via the mobile app, phone banking, or by calling the 24x7 card-blocking helpline. A replacement card is dispatched within 5–7 working days. The customer is not liable for unauthorised transactions reported within 3 working days of the fraudulent transaction, per the bank's Zero Liability policy for promptly reported fraud; liability is limited for reports made between 4–7 working days, and assessed case-by-case beyond that.

## Unauthorised transactions

Any customer report of a transaction they do not recognise is treated as a potential fraud case and must be escalated immediately to the Fraud team — see the Risk Taxonomy. The associate should not attempt to troubleshoot or reassure the customer that "it's probably fine" before escalation.

## International usage

Debit cards are enabled for international ATM and POS usage by default unless the customer has opted out. International transactions attract the foreign transaction fee published in the current Fee Schedule, charged in INR after currency conversion at the card network's prevailing rate on the settlement date (which may be 1–2 days after the transaction date).
