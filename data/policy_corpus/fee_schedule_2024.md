# Fee Schedule (2024 edition)

*Synthetic policy document created for the BankAssist Advisor capstone project. Does not represent any real bank's actual fees.*

This schedule lists standard fees for retail savings accounts, debit cards, and credit cards, effective 01 Jan 2024. **This version was superseded by the 2026 edition (see `fee-schedule-2026.1`) and is retained here only to test retrieval-versioning behaviour — it must never be cited for a current-date query.**

## Savings account fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Account maintenance (below minimum balance) | ₹450 / quarter |
| Minimum balance requirement | ₹10,000 |
| Duplicate statement (per request) | ₹100 |
| Cheque book (first 25 leaves/year) | Free; ₹4/leaf thereafter |
| Account closure (within 12 months of opening) | ₹500 |

## Debit card fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Annual maintenance fee | ₹250 + GST |
| Replacement card | ₹200 |
| ATM withdrawal — own bank | Free |
| ATM withdrawal — other bank (beyond 5 free/month) | ₹20 per transaction |
| International transaction (POS/ATM) | 3.0% of transaction value |

## Credit card fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Joining fee | ₹500 + GST (waived for first-year-free offers) |
| Annual fee | ₹1,999 + GST |
| Late payment fee | ₹450 flat, if minimum due unpaid by due date |
| Cash advance fee | 2.5% of amount withdrawn, min ₹300 |
| Foreign transaction fee | 3.0% of transaction value, converted at prevailing card-network rate |
| Over-limit fee | ₹600 |
| Interest on revolving balance | 3.4% per month (approx. 41% p.a.) |

## Effective date

These fees are effective from **01 January 2024** and remain in force until superseded by a later version of this schedule.
